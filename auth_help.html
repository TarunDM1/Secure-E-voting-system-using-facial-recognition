🔐 Password Assistance

Forgot your password?

For security, password resets are handled through verified channels.
Why this process?
E-Voting systems must prevent unauthorized access. Reset requests are verified by the administrator.
Tip: If you can still log in on another device, update your password from your profile (if your system supports it).

How to regain access

Follow the steps below to reset your account securely.
Support
1
Contact the Election Admin
Reach out to the admin and request a password reset.
2
Share verification details
Provide any of the following so your account can be verified:
  • Registered email address
  • National ID (used during registration)
  • Any voter reference/registration number (if applicable)
3
Receive a new password
After verification, the admin will reset your password. Log in and change it if your system allows.
Security note: Never share your password with anyone. The admin will reset it — they should not ask for your current password.
